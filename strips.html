Flight Strip Generator

Generate randomized nonradar flight progress strips and post them to the VKS, MHZ, and SQS bays. Hover over a strip to enlarge it, click to select it to mark it up, drag it around to another spot, or press F while selected to flag it. Every scenario is checked to be workable, can be graded, and is given a code you can use to load the same scenario later or give to someone else to have them work as the Remote.
